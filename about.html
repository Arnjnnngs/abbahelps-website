About ABBA Helps

To Rebuild Home, Restore Hope, and Transform Lives — for the most underserved survivors in the nation.

Our Story

Founded in 2015 on a simple conviction

ABBA Helps was founded by Don and Linabelle Finnegan as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to protecting and restoring survivors of human trafficking. What began as a mission to care for unseen and abused children has grown into a safe, nurturing refuge for trafficked pregnant teens, young mothers, and children.

We are passionate about this work because survivors remain one of the most underserved groups in the nation, with far too few dedicated beds and resources available.

Our Properties

Two homes in Missouri

Mountain Grove, MO — Children’s Ranch

A 265-acre Children’s Ranch envisioned as a community of professional, trauma-informed homes for child survivors of trafficking. The first home, Don’s Home, is licensed to foster and adopt trafficked children under 12 — the first such home in the state.

Lee’s Summit, MO — Safe House

A secure, private safe house licensed in December 2021, providing shelter, care, and support for pregnant trafficked teens and their babies — a nurturing environment with the skills and support to build a better path.

Why We Do What We Do

The reasons behind the mission

1 in 6 trafficked children reported are under age 12 — the youngest victims less than one year old.

The average age of entry into commercial sexual exploitation is roughly 12 to 14 years old.

About 1,000 American children are arrested for prostitution each year.

An estimated 100,000 American children are involved in human trafficking.

There are only about 300 beds in the United States to accommodate these children.

Child trafficking is widely unacknowledged and underserved — and we exist to change that.
