About Us

Here at ABBA Helps, we are driven to help child survivors of human trafficking. In America today, these children often go unnoticed. With an estimated 100,000 American children involved in trafficking and only 300 beds specifically geared to child survivors, we exist to improve the odds by adding more solutions for victims.

As a child-placing agency we train and license foster homes that are well-equipped to care for survivors of trafficking, with a focus on healing and permanency. We desire that ABBA Helps Children’s Ranch be the last stop for children before their permanent home — which is why adoption is a critical part of our mission.
