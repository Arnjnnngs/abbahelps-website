Our Mission

Rebuild Home. Restore Hope. Transform Lives.

We exist to help child survivors of human trafficking — one of the most underserved groups in the nation — find safety, healing, and a permanent, loving family.

Rebuild Home

Trauma-informed residential homes where survivors live as part of a safe, loving family — the foundation of all healing.

Restore Hope

Medical and prenatal care, licensed therapists, education, and spiritual support to help every survivor heal deeply.

Transform Lives

Life-skills, stability, and a path to permanency — so each young mother and child can step into a future filled with promise.

Child Placing & Adoption Agency

A licensed refuge — the first of its kind in Missouri

As a licensed child-placing agency, we train and license foster homes equipped to care for survivors of trafficking, then walk with each child toward healing and permanency.

Our home in Mountain Grove is licensed to foster and adopt trafficked children under 12 — the first home in the state for trafficked children. Our Lee’s Summit home cares for pregnant trafficked teens and their babies.
