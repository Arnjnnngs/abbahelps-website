31 Days Praying for Freedom

A prayer journey for rescue, healing, and restoration. By Linabelle.

A Devotional Journey

Prayer, hope, and freedom in Christ

Human trafficking is one of the darkest realities in our world today. But darkness does not have the final word. As followers of Christ, we are called not only to see — but to respond; not only to grieve — but to pray.

This devotional is a 31-day journey of intercession for the vulnerable, the exploited, the missing, the brokenhearted, and those fighting for justice and restoration. Each day includes Scripture, reflection, prayer, and declarations to help you stand in the gap.

31 Days of Freedom (PDF) Prayer Freedom — Long Version (PDF)
Heavenly Father, I come before You in the mighty and saving name of Jesus Christ. You are the God who sees the oppressed, hears the cries of the brokenhearted, and brings freedom to those held in bondage. Over these next 31 days, draw me closer to Your heart. Teach me to pray with compassion, courage, and unwavering faith. Bring freedom where there is bondage, light where there is darkness, healing where there is trauma, and hope where despair has taken root. Protect the vulnerable. Strengthen survivors. Break the chains that only You can break. For whom the Son sets free is free indeed. In Jesus’ Name, Amen.
